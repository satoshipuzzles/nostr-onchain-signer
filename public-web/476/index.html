OpenBitcoin Knots #476· updated October 30, 2026

1-year rolling coinbase maturity. No cliff.

Knots 29.4.3, released October 28, holds each mining reward 180 days (#468), but only for rewards mined before RDTS ends in September 2027. #476 now builds on top of it: from block 995,000, every reward mined since 979,919 waits a full year from its own block, until new coins run out.

What improves

A full year, not 180 days

Hashrate rented to grab rewards and sell has to wait a year to get paid. That's 52,560 rewards locked at any moment instead of 25,920, and miners stay tied to the chain's long-term health.

Doesn't end with RDTS

Under #468, rewards mined after RDTS ends (September 2027) go back to 100 blocks. Here every reward keeps its year until new coins run out. Debates about RDTS no longer move miner payouts.

One reward per block

Each reward unlocks at its own block + 52,560, so newly mined coins reach the market at a steady pace with no release date to dump into. The last one unlocks at block 6,982,559.

Never re-locks a spendable reward

It switches on at block 995,000, while #468 still holds every reward it covers. Nothing that has become spendable is ever locked again, and the 45-day window's rewards are left as 29.4.3 has them.

Safe to run before it switches on

Until block 995,000 it accepts exactly the same blocks as 29.4.3, so nodes running it and nodes running 29.4.3 stay on the same chain. That leaves about three months for review.

A soft fork

It only rejects spends 29.4.3 allows, so non-upgraded nodes stay on the same chain. Weakening it later would take a hard fork, which is deliberate.

As of October 30. #477, #478 and #479 are add-ons to #468. #483 only keeps the 45-day window's rewards locked for 6,480 blocks in consensus, so they aren't all released at 979,920; it doesn't cover later rewards. #429's relay policy also holds rewards a year with no end, but its consensus lock ends at RDTS expiry.

The latest

  • #468 shipped. Knots 29.4.3, released October 28, holds every reward mined from block 979,919 for 180 days, rolling, until RDTS ends.
  • #476 now builds on it (October 30 update). From block 995,000, every reward mined since 979,919 waits a full year from its own block. Until then it accepts exactly the same blocks as 29.4.3. MarcanoFilms' end point (~2140) is kept.
  • What changed: the earlier version started at block 979,920 and also re-locked the 45-day window's rewards for a year, which drew the NACKs. The new version leaves those rewards alone and never re-locks anything spendable. The earlier version is kept at the branch rolling-1y-pre-fallback-83a1f68.
  • The 45-day window: even in 29.4.3, those rewards can all be spent from block 979,920 under consensus rules; relay still makes each one wait 6,480 blocks. #483 is the separate fix for that.

Review so far

  • 14 ACK. Tested: Kilombino (regtest and a pruned mainnet node, re-tested with the end point), webtrekker95-hue. Concept: Daamir-Al-Adel, MarcanoFilms, papier-krieger, Ari4ka, Antonbtc2020, keyandchain21, chainboarder, BrassMonkey87, stackitdeep, FreezyMcFlurry, b2bpleb, himecase-debug. These were given on the earlier version; the tested ACKs need a re-test on the new one.
  • 2 NACK: SeventyEi8ht (objected to re-locking rewards already mined in the 45-day window, which the new version no longer does), mynpub.
  • MarcanoFilms mutation-tested the rule and made the case that a full year keeps 52,560 rewards locked at any moment, twice as many as #468's 25,920.
  • stackitdeep prefers it to #468 because keeping the lock separate from RDTS gives more flexibility.
  • FlyTheElephant1 pointed out that shortening a permanent lock later would be a hard fork (agreed, and deliberate), and has asked for a written wind-down plan in case one is ever needed.
  • On #468, luke-jr, the Knots maintainer, posted a Concept NACK, preferring to let the 45 days expire and handle any longer lock as an ordinary soft fork. GregTonoski and SeventyEi8ht now take the same view.

What it means

  • #468 has more support, about 2.4 times as many ACKs, and it shipped in 29.4.3.
  • The two groups barely overlap: 5 people back both. Most #476 supporters (9 of 14) have not ACKed #468.
  • No one with an ACK on #468 has NACKed #476. The strongest objection on both, luke-jr's and GregTonoski's "let it expire", is to any longer lock, not to #476 in particular.

Notes

  • The 29.4.2 release that shipped the 45-day lock said maturity could go “up to 350 days” and put miners on notice they may need to wait much longer.
  • RDTS expires September 1, 2027. Block 995,000 is about seven months earlier, so #468 is still in force when #476 switches on.
  • 52,560 blocks is a year at the 10-minute target; at the recent pace of about 9.3 minutes per block it's closer to 339 days.
  • What goes into a Knots release is up to the Knots maintainers.
