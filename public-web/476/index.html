OpenBitcoin Knots #476· updated October 26, 2026

1-year rolling coinbase maturity. No cliff.

Under #419, about 6,400 mining rewards all become spendable in one block, 979,920. This pull request gives every reward its own one-year clock instead.

What improves

No supply cliff

No block where thousands of rewards unlock at once. Newly mined coins reach the market at a steady one reward per block, so there's no single moment to dump into.

Hit-and-run hashing costs more

Hashrate rented to grab rewards and sell has to wait a year to get paid. The chain has a year to respond, and miners stay tied to its long-term health.

Predictable for every miner

Each reward unlocks at its own block + 52,560. No expiry dates, no dependence on future votes, nothing to guess. Coverage ends only when new coins run out, by the height a reward was mined at.

Separate from RDTS

Debates about extending or ending RDTS no longer move miner payouts. The lock reads block heights only.

One rule for every reward

Every reward from 973,440 waits the same year from its own block, with no shorter lock for early miners.

A soft fork

It only rejects spends the old rules allowed, so non-upgraded nodes stay on the same chain. Weakening it later would take a hard fork, which is deliberate.

As of October 26. #477, #478 and #479 are add-ons to #468 and need it merged first. #483 only keeps the 45-day window's rewards locked for 6,480 blocks in consensus, so they aren't all released at 979,920; it doesn't cover later rewards. #429's relay policy also holds rewards a year with no end, but its consensus lock ends at RDTS expiry.

The latest

  • The end point is now in #476. MarcanoFilms' change was merged: the lock covers every reward mined until block 6,930,000, the first block with no new coins (around 2140). It ends by the height a reward was mined at, so every covered reward still waits its full year. The last one unlocks at block 6,982,559, one reward per block, with no cliff.
  • The newest community test build, rc5, carries #468 (180 days, ends with RDTS). Kilombino briefly put up a test build with #476 instead, then took it down, saying testers are going with #468. These are test builds, not official releases.
  • If #476 misses block 979,920, a fallback takes over. It is built and tested: the same rolling 52,560-block year, switched on before block 1,005,839, so nothing that has become spendable is ever re-locked.

Review so far

  • 13 ACK. Tested: Kilombino (regtest and a pruned mainnet node, re-tested with the end point), webtrekker95-hue. Concept: Daamir-Al-Adel, MarcanoFilms, papier-krieger, Ari4ka, Antonbtc2020, keyandchain21, chainboarder, BrassMonkey87, stackitdeep, FreezyMcFlurry, b2bpleb.
  • 2 NACK: SeventyEi8ht (objects to re-locking rewards already mined in the 45-day window), mynpub.
  • MarcanoFilms mutation-tested the rule and made the case that a full year keeps 52,560 rewards locked at any moment, twice as many as #468's 25,920.
  • stackitdeep prefers it to #468 because keeping the lock separate from RDTS gives more flexibility.
  • FlyTheElephant1 pointed out that shortening a permanent lock later would be a hard fork (agreed, and deliberate), and has asked for a written wind-down plan in case one is ever needed.
  • On #468, luke-jr, the Knots maintainer, posted a Concept NACK: he would rather let the 45 days expire and handle any longer lock as an ordinary soft fork. GregTonoski and SeventyEi8ht now take the same view.

Notes

  • The 29.4.2 release that shipped the 45-day lock said maturity could go “up to 350 days” and put miners on notice they may need to wait much longer.
  • Default relay policy already makes each reward wait 6,480 confirmations from its own block, so today's cliff is only reachable by a miner including the spends in its own block. #476 closes that in consensus.
  • 52,560 blocks is a year at the 10-minute target; at the recent pace of about 9.3 minutes per block it's closer to 339 days.
  • Release candidates are community test builds. The final release contents are up to the Knots maintainers.
